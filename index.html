OUR MEDICAL PROVIDERS

Board-Certified Clinicians Dedicated To Portsmouth

Our medical staff combines clinical rigor with deep empathy. We take the time to answer every question, explain diagnostic results clearly, and formulate treatment plans tailored to your specific health circumstances.

Physician Assistant

Aaron Sents, PA-C

Physician Assistant - Certified

Aaron Sents is a board-certified Physician Assistant bringing over 18 years of clinical expertise across primary medicine, internal medicine, and urgent care.

Aaron graduated from the prestigious Physician Assistant program at Wichita State University. He is deeply committed to patient empowerment, proactive preventative screenings, and comprehensive chronic disease management for hypertension, diabetes, and cardiovascular health.
Adult Nurse Practitioner

Laurie Salerno, ANP-BC

Adult Nurse Practitioner - Board Certified

Laurie Salerno has devoted over 25 years to adult healthcare, specializing in routine adult medicine, geriatric care, and personalized disease management.

Laurie earned her Master of Science in Nursing from Virginia Commonwealth University. She champions holistic, patient-centered care that emphasizes preventative screenings, nutrition, mental wellbeing, and continuity of care for adults at every life transition.
Family Nurse Practitioner

Shanice Edwards, NP-BC

Family Nurse Practitioner - Board Certified

Shanice Edwards brings dynamic diagnostic acumen and heart-centered clinical advocacy to the Hampton Roads community, specializing in preventive family medicine.

Shanice completed her advanced practice nursing credentials with highest honors. Her clinical passions include comprehensive women’s health, pediatric-to-adult transitions, acute infection management, and compassionate patient education.
